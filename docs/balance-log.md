# Balance Log

## Starting posture

The current rules aim for:

- no immortal soup
- no instant extinction under presets
- no single obvious god stat
- readable trait shifts under each preset
- scheduled scenarios that feel intentional rather than chaotic

## Current tuning principles

- Food scarcity favors efficiency and punishes body cost.
- Predation pressure favors speed and camouflage.
- Cold stress mildly favors size and efficiency.
- Habitat visibility strongly favors camouflage and mildly punishes obvious size.
- Scheduled events should create a visible turn in the story, not a full reset of the simulation.
- Built-in scheduled scenarios should use a small number of legible events rather than a long script.

## Current scenario set

- Static scenarios
  - Balanced World
  - Scarcity Crunch
  - Predator Surge
  - Exposed Habitat
  - Cold Snap
- Scheduled scenarios
  - Predator Pulse
  - Cold Snap Recovery

## Current tuning notes

- Predator Pulse now keeps background scarcity lower so the predator script stays visually and analytically dominant after the spike.
- Cold Snap Recovery now reads more like a lean winter followed by relief, so thrift matters more than raw camouflage during the harsh phase.
- Scheduled-event summaries now call out the biggest pressure changes first instead of whichever override fields happened to be listed first.
- The diversity pass now adds a small phenotype crowding adjustment so overrepresented body plans lose a little edge instead of collapsing the late game into the same monoculture every time.
- Low diversity now keys off the diversity score rather than a single raw-variance cutoff, and recovery can register across a short multi-generation rebound instead of needing a one-step spike.
- Balanced World now stays more open through the back half, Predator Pulse reads as a chart-visible squeeze and rebound, and Cold Snap Recovery is the main scenario that should surface an explicit diversity rebound marker.
- Scarcity Crunch now pushes harder toward thrift and downsizing instead of quietly drifting into the same camouflage-heavy outcome as exposure-driven worlds.
- Predator Surge now favors speed more clearly than the other static presets, while Cold Snap leans more toward sturdier, thriftier survivors instead of ending as another fast-stealth population.
- Exposed Habitat now does the clearest camouflage teaching job in the static set, so the built-in worlds land on more distinct late-run body plans.

## What to watch during future tuning

- whether energy efficiency quietly dominates too many presets
- whether size becomes either a trap or a universal winner
- whether predation pressure and habitat visibility feel too similar
- whether summaries still match the actual chart and sprite outcomes
- whether scheduled-event summaries feel too repetitive or too vague
- whether scenario timelines become noisy once multiple emergent and scheduled markers stack together
- whether diversity rebounds remain readable if we later raise population ceilings or add more aggressive scheduled worlds
